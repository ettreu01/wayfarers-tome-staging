Conditions & Notes

Attack Planner

How to attack: Choose your weapon, decide with your DM whether advantage or Sneak Attack applies, then roll. The app calculates dice but cannot decide whether a creature is surprised.

Choose the weapon and roll mode. Sneak Attack and surprise must be confirmed at the table; this tool does not determine eligibility.

Choose your attack options and roll.

On a natural 20 or a qualifying Assassinate hit against a surprised creature, damage dice are doubled. Damage modifiers are not doubled. A natural 1 misses regardless of modifiers. Surprise does not automatically grant advantage; 2014 Assassinate grants advantage against creatures that have not taken a turn yet.

Death Save Roller

At 0 HP? Death saves are normally made at the start of your turn. Three successes stabilize you; three failures mean death. Damage at 0 HP can also cause failures.

Roll a death saving throw at 0 HP. Natural 20 restores 1 HP; natural 1 counts as two failures.

Active Conditions

Condition tracking is a reminder, not an automated rules engine. The Exhaustion option is a flag only.

Learn to Play

2014 D&D 5e rules · Your DM makes the final call.

My Turn

New to combat? You can move and take an action on your turn. You may also have a bonus action, and you can interact with one object for free. Your reaction can happen on another creature's turn if triggered. Checking a box is only a reminder, not a rules restriction.

Movement can be split before and after your action. Reactions normally refresh at the start of your next turn. Sneak Attack can be used once per turn, not once per round.

What Can I Do?

Quick Dice Reference

Ability checks

Roll d20 + relevant ability modifier + proficiency bonus if proficient. Expertise doubles the proficiency bonus.

Advantage / disadvantage

Roll two d20s and use the higher result for advantage, or the lower for disadvantage. They cancel if both apply.

Attack vs. damage

First roll to hit against Armor Class. If the attack hits, roll the weapon's damage dice and add appropriate modifiers. A natural 20 is a critical hit.

Rules reminder: Hiding requires a suitable situation and a Dexterity (Stealth) check as determined by your DM. Being hidden is not automatic advantage in every circumstance.

Origins

Kiran Silverbough is a 106-year-old Wood Elf from Sylvaeren. His parents, Caelan and Liora Silverbough, disappeared on an expedition when he was 62. His grandfather Thalion raised him afterward.

At age 100, Kiran secretly left his community to escape the expectations of its elders. After six years on the road, his travels have brought him to Bellwether's centennial survival festival.

He is stealthy, sarcastic, quick with a quip, and happiest when he can keep his distance and let his bow do the talking.
